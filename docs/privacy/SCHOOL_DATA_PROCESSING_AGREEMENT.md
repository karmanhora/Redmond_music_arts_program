# Proposed School Technology-Use / Data-Processing Agreement (DRAFT)

> **DRAFT for district legal review. Do not sign without advice.** If the operator
> is a minor, a **parent or guardian must be involved** before any liability,
> indemnity, or term commitment is signed. The district may have its own required
> form; if so, use the district's form. This draft intentionally does **not**
> promise that the school has no liability or that the developer has none.

**Parties.** (1) Lake Washington School District / Redmond High School ("the
District"); (2) [DEVELOPER NAME] ("the Provider"), who operates the RHS Music &
Arts Attendance Tracker ("the App").

## 1. Purpose & scope
The District authorizes the Provider to operate the App to record attendance for
the approved program(s) at [SCHOOL/PROGRAM]. The Provider acts as the District's
service provider for this function. The App is free of charge.

## 2. Roles
- **District:** determines which records may be entered, which users are
  authorized, retention decisions for its records, and the educational purpose.
- **Provider:** operates, hosts and maintains the App; accesses student data only
  as needed to provide and support the service.

## 3. Authorized data & use limits
The Provider will process only the data listed in
[`DATA_INVENTORY_AND_FLOWS.md`](DATA_INVENTORY_AND_FLOWS.md), only for the
attendance purpose. The Provider will **not**: sell student personal information;
use it for targeted advertising; build student profiles for non-educational
purposes; or use it for any purpose inconsistent with this Agreement.

## 4. Security
The Provider will maintain reasonable administrative, technical and physical
safeguards, including the controls described in
[`SECURITY_AND_INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md`](SECURITY_AND_INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md), and will
use service providers bound to equivalent obligations (see
[`THIRD_PARTY_PROVIDERS.md`](THIRD_PARTY_PROVIDERS.md)).

## 5. Access, correction, deletion
The Provider will facilitate District access to, and correction of, records, and
will delete student personal information within [N] days of a District request,
except where retention is required by law or the District asks to keep it.
Account deletion within the App is described in
[`RETENTION_AND_DELETION.md`](RETENTION_AND_DELETION.md).

## 6. Incident response
The Provider will notify the District **without undue delay and in any event
promptly** after discovering a suspected breach, and will cooperate so the
District can meet RCW 19.255 (30-day notice; AG notice if >500 residents).
**[counsel to assign responsibility]**

## 7. FERPA & Washington law
The parties intend the Provider to be treated, if legally permissible, as a
"school official" with a legitimate educational interest under 34 CFR
99.31(a)(1)(i)(B), performing a function the District would otherwise staff, and
under the District's direct control. The District is responsible for its own
FERPA notice and disclosure. If the App is a "school service", the Provider will
comply with RCW 28A.604. **[counsel]**

## 8. Ownership
Software and source code remain the Provider's property (see
[`OWNERSHIP_AND_SCHOOL_USE_PERMISSION.md`](OWNERSHIP_AND_SCHOOL_USE_PERMISSION.md)).
Student data belongs to the District/students.

## 9. Costs, support, termination
The App is provided free. Support is provided on a best-effort basis by
[CONTACT]. On termination, the Provider will [return/export] the District's data
as agreed and then delete it, subject to the retention schedule.

## 10. Liability
**[Requires negotiation and counsel; and parental/guardian review if the
Provider is a minor.]** The parties should agree on liability appropriate to a
free service, without clauses that purport to eliminate either party's legal
duties.
