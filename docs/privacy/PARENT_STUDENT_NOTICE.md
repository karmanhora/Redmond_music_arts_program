# Parent / Guardian & Student Notice (DRAFT)

> **DRAFT.** Only use if the district requires a notice; the school/district owns
> student records and may have its own language. **Not legal advice.**

[School letterhead / district-approved header]

Dear Parent/Guardian and Student,

The [PROGRAM NAME] at Redmond High School uses a web app, the **RHS Music & Arts
Attendance Tracker**, to record attendance at rehearsals, performances, and other
events.

**What it records:** the student's name, email (for sign-in), program and section,
and whether they attended each event (including late/excused status). Staff may
add a short note.

**Who can see it:** program staff and, where relevant, the student's own section
leader. Students see their own attendance. **Other programs cannot see this
program's data.**

**How it is protected:** sign-in is required; access is enforced by the database;
data is encrypted in transit.

**No advertising.** The app shows no ads, sells no information, and does not use
it for any purpose other than running the program.

**Questions or requests.** To inspect, correct, or request deletion of your
student's records, contact the school's records office and reference LWSD Policy
3231. For questions about the app, contact [CONTACT EMAIL].
